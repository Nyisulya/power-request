🕊️ About Power Request Portal Kuhusu Power Request Portal

The Power Request Portal is a global digital sanctuary designed to connect believers across borders. Our mission is to bridge language and geographical barriers, allowing members of our international prayer group to submit prayer requests, share testimonies, and join live worship sessions in their own preferred language.

Every prayer request and testimony submitted is automatically translated in real-time between English and Swahili, ensuring that a believer in Tanzania and another in the United States or Europe can pray together, read the same praise reports, and stay united in spirit.

Power Request Portal ni madhabahu ya kidijitali ya kimataifa iliyoundwa kuunganisha waumini kuvuka mipaka. Dhamira yetu ni kuvunja vizuizi vya lugha na kijiografia, kuwezesha washiriki wa kikundi chetu cha maombi cha kimataifa kutuma maombi yao, kushuhudia matendo makuu ya Mungu, na kuungana kwenye ibada za moja kwa moja kwa lugha wanayoielewa vizuri zaidi.

Kila ombi la maombi na ushuhuda unaotumwa unatafsiriwa moja kwa moja kati ya Kiswahili na Kiingereza, ikihakikisha kuwa mwamini wa Tanzania na mwingine aliyepo Marekani au Ulaya wanaweza kuomba kwa pamoja, kusoma shuhuda zilezile, na kubaki wamoja katika Roho.
